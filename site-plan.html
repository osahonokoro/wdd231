Smart Aquaculture Hub

Website Project Site Plan

Site Name

Smart Aquaculture Hub — This name reflects the focus on intelligent monitoring and energy‑efficient water filtration in aquaculture. It communicates innovation, sustainability, and technology integration.

Optional domain: smartaquaculturehub.org

Site Purpose

The site provides information about smart aquaculture systems that integrate IoT sensors, machine learning, and energy‑efficient water filtration. It educates visitors on system design, showcases results and benefits, and promotes sustainable fish farming practices.

Scenarios

  • How can smart aquaculture systems reduce energy costs compared to traditional filtration methods?
  • What sensors and hardware are needed to build an IoT‑enabled aquaculture monitoring system?
  • Can machine learning really predict water quality trends in fish farming?

Color Scheme

  • Deep Blue (#004080) — header, footer, navigation background.
  • Aqua/Teal (#00b3b3) — buttons, highlights, active states.
  • Green (#4CAF50) — call‑to‑action elements, sustainability highlights.
  • Light Gray (#f5f5f5) — page background.
  • Dark Gray (#333) — body text.
  • Purple (#78247C) with Teal (#003838) — accessible CTA combo.

Typography

  • Roboto Bold — headings.
  • Arial Regular — body text.
  • Roboto Medium — buttons and accents.
